Champions

Battle victories grant Gold + XP to your squad. Level Up spends banked XP + Gold. Level cap: — raise it with Shards (rare battle drops).

Summon

Main Boss victories pay Summon Shards. Spend them to summon a new champion — higher rarities are rarer. A pull that lands on a champion you already own refunds part of the cost instead of doing nothing. A bigger roster also raises your Roster Tier, which is what lets you push Tower/Faction Wars/Main Boss/Dungeon/Galactic War/Grand Arena past their current stage cap.

Arena Shop

Grand Arena victories pay Arena Medals. Spend them on an Arena Pull — a rolled gear piece (Epic or Legendary, random slot/stats) guaranteed to carry the Vanguard set, found nowhere else.

Farm Gear

Manual roll for testing — battle victories also drop gear now. Rolls a main stat + rarity-scaled substats.

Farm Charms

Manual roll for testing — battle victories also drop charms now.
